# Roadmap

What's coming to **Aegis: Forever Instance Journal**, and what's being thought about. Nothing
here is a promise: plans move as Forever changes and as players say what they need. Ideas and
votes are welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/Torchlite-bit/Aegis_Forever_Instance_Journal/issues)
or the Discord.

Size is a rough guess at the work: **S** a day or less, **M** a few days, **L** a week or more.

---

## Next

| What | Why | Size |
|---|---|:---:|
| **Boss tracking on the dungeon map** | The boss you're fighting marked on its pin (the one moment the game says where you are), the next boss to kill highlighted, and a clearer "You are here". | M |
| **Keys, attunements and hidden bosses for every dungeon** | Before you go, filled in one dungeon at a time: the classic keys (the Scarlet Key, the Workshop Key, the Crescent Key, the Key to the City...), attunements, and any hidden boss to prepare for. | M |
| **Map icons on or off inside dungeons** | The world map legend's switches done; next, the same for the journal's own dungeon maps (boss pins, quest marks, doors to other floors). | S |
| **Turn the Gear Finder off** *(player request)* | For players who don't want it: no tab, no character panel tab, no stat weight lines or arrows, no Pawn link. | S |
| **The Record tab, hidden or in the settings** *(player request)* | Most players never record. Hide the tab, with its controls (start, stop, combat log) in the options instead. | S |
| **A settings page inside the journal** *(player request)* | The options above in one place, reachable from the journal itself as well as the game's AddOns options. | M |
| **Where it drops** | Every item's tooltip names the dungeon and boss that drop it, wherever you see the item. | S |
| **Maps of the caves outside dungeons** | Blizzard's own drawn maps of the way in to Wailing Caverns, Gnomeregan, Uldaman, Maraudon (both caves) and Blackrock Mountain, shown when you're there, with your arrow and the dungeon doors. | M |
| **Scarlet Monastery's entrance hall** | Blizzard's newer map of the hall redrawn for the classic four wings: the passages to the Cathedral and the Armory drawn back in, a door for each wing, and a sketch of a Scarlet warrior in the empty parchment. | M |
| **Dock the window like Blizzard's** *(player request)* | The player's choice: docked at the top left, sliding aside when another window opens there, as the game's own panels do, or free to move as now. Must not cause errors in combat. | M |
| **Forever's new dungeons** | They open on 4 November: their placeholders filled in from recorded runs, one dungeon at a time. | L |

## Later

| What | Why | Size |
|---|---|:---:|
| **Controller support** *(player request)* | Moving through the journal with a gamepad. First find out how Forever's own gamepad mode moves through Blizzard's windows, and whether ConsolePort players need anything different. | L |
| **Dungeon maps that follow you** | Inside a dungeon the game still names the room you're in: the map switches to that room's floor as you go deeper, and "You are here" works in the classic dungeons too. The rooms come from the game's own data; dungeons rebuilt in later expansions (Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep, Scholomance) need checking by hand. | M |
| **Rare spawn markers** | Where rare creatures spawn, on the world map and inside dungeons, with what they drop, and their own switch in the map legend and filter menu. Marked with the game's own small map icons ([Wowpedia's list of them](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Wowpedia:List_of_small_icons)). | L |
| **The raids** | Filled in as each opens on Forever; they show *Coming soon* until then. | L |
| **Native speakers' corrections** | Every drafted translation checked by someone who speaks it. | M |
| **Boats and portals still missing** | The routes no one has captured yet (the Valanaar–Skywatcher airship, a few ferries). | S |

## Ideas to talk about

Not planned yet. Say which ones you'd use.

| Idea | What it would do | Size |
|---|---|:---:|
| **More sketches on the maps** | Drawings in the maps' empty parchment, like Blizzard's own: the Ashbringer is drawn and waiting, kept off the Scarlet Monastery map for now so it stays uncluttered. | S |
| **Loot filters for you** | On a loot page: upgrades only (by the Gear Finder's weights), only what your class can use, only recipes for your professions. | S |
| **A wishlist** | The ★ favourites gathered into one list across every dungeon, with a note when one drops or comes up for a roll. | M |
| **Quests to pick up first** | For a dungeon: the quests worth having before going in, and where their givers stand, so nobody arrives without them. | M |
| **What to run next** | One suggestion that weighs the quests you have, the upgrades waiting and your level together, not upgrades alone. | M |
| **Tactics to party chat** | A button on a boss page that posts a one-line summary of the fight, for groups new to it. | S |
| **Who is it an upgrade for?** | With others in the group who run the journal, see whose upgrade a drop would be before rolling. | L |
| **Run history** | Your recorded runs listed: when, how long, which bosses, what dropped. | M |
| **Reputation goals** | How many more runs to the next standing with a dungeon's faction. | S |
| **Window size and text size** | A scale for the journal's window, and larger text. | S |
| **Report a mistake** | A button that gathers what's on screen into a note to paste in the Discord or on GitHub, so a wrong drop or place is easy to report. | S |
| **A launcher for bar add-ons** | The journal on Titan Panel, ChocolateBar and the like, besides the minimap button. | S |
