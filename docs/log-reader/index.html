Add your combat log to your recording

Addons can't see what bosses cast inside Forever's dungeons, but the game's combat log can. Paste the recording you copied from the journal's Record tab, add the log file, and send the result in our Discord: one paste with everything.

1 Paste your recording

In game, open the journal's Record tab, press Copy, then Ctrl+C. Paste it here.

2 Add your combat log

It's in the game's Logs folder, named for when it started, like WoWCombatLog-101126_172405.txt. The journal writes one per dungeon while Write the combat log in dungeons is ticked.

What this page does

It reads the log in your browser: the file never leaves your computer. From the boss fights only, it keeps what the enemies cast: each spell, how hard it hit, when it was first cast and how often. It leaves out everything else, including every player's name, so your group isn't shared.

Part of the Aegis Instance Journal. The lines it adds are read by the journal's build tool, Tools/build_data.py.
