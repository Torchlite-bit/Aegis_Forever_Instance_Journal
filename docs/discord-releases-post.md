# Discord: the #releases post

The text of the journal's post in the Discord server's **#releases** forum: what it is, how to
help, and where to get it. That post belongs to the Aegis Tools bot, so neither GitHub nor the
release workflow can change it: paste the block below into it by hand, or update it with the
Aegis Discord tools from the owner's PC. Each version's changelog goes to the journal's post in
**#changelogs** by itself (`Tools/discord_post.py`). Keep it under Discord's 2,000 characters.

```text
## ⚔️ Aegis: Forever Instance Journal
Retail's instance journal and dungeon maps, brought to WoW: Forever. Press **Shift+J**.

**What's in it**
📖 **35 dungeons** and **3 raids coming soon**: bosses, loot, abilities, 3D models and quests
🗺️ **Dungeon maps** with boss pins; the bosses you've killed are crossed out
🧭 **World map**: every dungeon door (click to open it, right-click for a waypoint), flight masters, boats, zeppelins, quest **!** / **?** marks, **zone level ranges**, and **unexplored areas** on request
🛡️ **Reputation**: every faction's rewards by standing. Captured quartermasters show their exact costs; the rest are marked unconfirmed
🎯 **Gear Finder**: your best dungeon upgrades, with stat weights to edit or bring from Pawn
🔍 **Search** across everything
🇷🇺 **In Russian** on a Russian client, thanks to **Staree4ok**
🔴 **Record tab** and **Log Reader**: help us fill in Forever's journal

**How to help**
1. Leave **Record automatically in dungeons** ticked on the Record tab
2. Run a dungeon and open the loot on every boss
3. Record tab → **Copy**, then paste it into the Log Reader with your combat log: <https://torchlite-bit.github.io/Aegis_Forever_Instance_Journal/log-reader/>
4. Paste the result in a post in **#share-and-submit** (tag: Instance Journal)
At a quartermaster, open their shop and press **Capture vendor** on the Reputation tab, then Copy and post it the same way.
Your name goes on the journal's contributors list. 🙏

**Download:** <https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/aegis-forever-instance-journal> or <https://github.com/Torchlite-bit/Aegis_Forever_Instance_Journal/releases>
Put the `Aegis_Forever_Instance_Journal` folder in `_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns`, then restart the game fully.
What's new: <https://github.com/Torchlite-bit/Aegis_Forever_Instance_Journal/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md>

🗺️ Forever's dungeon maps are by **Santiago Reyes**. Support his work: <https://www.patreon.com/c/SantiagoReyesMaps>
```
